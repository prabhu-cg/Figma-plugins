8. PUBLISH  (you)
-----------------
1. Figma desktop > Plugins > Manage plugins > find DSLog (development) > Publish.
2. Fill the form with section 6; upload section 5 assets.
3. Choose who can use it: "Anyone" (public Community listing).
4. Accept the Community Terms and submit for review.
5. Review typically takes several business days. Watch the email on your Figma
   account for approval or change requests.
6. After approval: install it from the Community page on a clean account and
   repeat the smoke test in section 4.

10. KNOWN LIMITS TO MENTION IF ASKED
------------------------------------
- DSLog only tracks what you tell it to; it does not watch the file in the
  background and does not read Figma's native Version History.
- The "impact index" (instance counts) is an explicit, optional full-document
  scan and can be slow on very large files.
- Selection in the Changes list is not remembered across a re-scan.
- Window opens at 1180x760. Below about 1100px wide the History detail panel
  drops beneath its list instead of sitting beside it.
